About Dr. Kanzaria

Dr. Kanzaria is a cardiologist with subspecialty training in interventional cardiology. After completing residency and both cardiology fellowships at Thomas Jefferson University Hospital, Dr. Kanzaria also earned a Master of Science in Healthcare Quality and Safety from Thomas Jefferson University. He is a Fellow of the American College of Cardiology (FACC).
