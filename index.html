About Dr. Kanzaria

Dr. Kanzaria is a cardiologist with subspecialty training in interventional cardiology, and a Fellow of the American College of Cardiology. He completed his residency, general cardiology fellowship, and interventional cardiology fellowship at Thomas Jefferson University Hospital, and holds a Master of Science in Healthcare Quality and Safety from the Jefferson College of Population Health.
