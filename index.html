My Approach to Care

My mission is to offer excellent treatment to my patients and their families.

I provide thorough evaluations for every kind of cardiovascular concern, with compassion and respect. I will always be honest with you about what I find and recommend, and we will talk through every option together, from lifestyle changes and medications to procedures when they are needed.

Dr. Kanzaria is a board-certified cardiologist with advanced training in interventional cardiology and a Fellow of the American College of Cardiology.
