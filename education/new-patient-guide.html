Filling this out before your first visit helps us focus on what matters most to you. Bring it with you, along with your insurance card and photo ID. You don't need to complete every line — bring whatever you have and we'll fill the gaps together.

Before I call or arrive

  • Locate my insurance card and photo ID.
  • List every medication, vitamin, and supplement I take (including low-dose aspirin).
  • Gather any recent blood-pressure readings or a home log.
  • Collect recent test results or reports from other providers if I have them.
  • Write down my questions.
  • Plan for a family member or friend to join me if helpful.
  • Confirm my appointment time, directions, and parking.
