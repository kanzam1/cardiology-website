Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website, why it is collected, and the choices you have. It applies to this website only. It is not a Notice of Privacy Practices, and it does not describe how your medical records are handled in the office or the hospital.

Please do not send medical information through this website

This site has no secure messaging and no patient portal. Do not use the newsletter signup, email, or any other part of this site to send symptoms, test results, medication lists, or any other personal health information. For medical questions, call the office at (908) 864-4027. In an emergency, call 911.

Information collected

This website does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect payment information. The only information you can actively give it is your email address, and only if you choose to subscribe to the newsletter.

What is collected and when
Information When Purpose
Email address Only if you submit the newsletter form To send the newsletter you asked for
Subscription status and signup date Created automatically when you subscribe To manage your subscription and honor unsubscribe requests
Technical request data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) Automatically, on every visit Standard web server logging by the hosting provider, used for security and reliability

This site does not run advertising trackers, and it does not build a profile of your browsing across other websites.

How your email address is used

If you subscribe, your email address is used to send the newsletter and occasional updates about the practice. It is not sold, rented, or traded. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask to be removed using the contact details below.

Subscription is confirmed by email, so you will not be added to the list unless you complete that confirmation step.

Service providers

A few outside companies help run this site. They receive only what is needed to perform their function.

  • MailerLite — stores the newsletter subscriber list and sends the emails. Your email address and subscription details are held there on the practice's behalf.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website and keeps standard server logs.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — protects the newsletter form from automated abuse. It may set cookies and collect device and interaction data to decide whether a submission is genuine.
  • Google Maps — the embedded maps on the home page load from Google and are subject to Google's own privacy terms.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not set its own cookies for advertising or analytics. Cookies may be set by the embedded third-party services described above, principally Google reCAPTCHA and Google Maps, when those elements load. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking them may prevent the newsletter form from submitting, because the spam check depends on them.

Legal bases and your choices

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, the newsletter is sent on the basis of your consent, which you give when you confirm your subscription and may withdraw at any time. Server logging rests on the legitimate interest of keeping the site secure and working.

You may ask to access, correct, or delete the email address held for you, or ask to stop receiving the newsletter, by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting the office. Requests are honored within a reasonable period, subject to any records the practice is required to keep by law.

Data retention

Newsletter subscriber information is kept until you unsubscribe or ask for it to be deleted. Server logs kept by the hosting provider are retained according to that provider's standard schedule.

Security

This site is served over an encrypted connection, and access to the subscriber list is limited to those who need it to send the newsletter. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, which is one more reason not to send health information through this site.

Children

This website is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and the newsletter is not offered to anyone under 18.

Links to other sites

Patient education pages may link to outside organizations, such as the American College of Cardiology. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site or the services behind it change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
